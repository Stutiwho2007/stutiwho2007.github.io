Original poetry · Shwetangi Srivastava

Love in the Digital Age

Two souls, Long distances, far-off lands Love bridging the two estranged bands But sadly, it’s all built on the internet With various boundaries already preset Sometimes busyness, sometimes tiredness Sometimes, the loving weighing a little less Sometimes connectivity issues, souls or network Different opinions collide like that of a spoon and fork Sometimes the urge to meet stronger, sometimes weak And a strong, tight hug till the bodies squeak You fall in love and fall out of love sometimes, and that’s fine Because the longer love stays, it ages well like a good old wine You spend time looking at each other through the screen Imagining to grow old together when the grass stays green Dreaming of a fictional world with just the two of you in it And counting the minutes till you meet slowly, bit by bit The times seem to move slowly while you love someone online Planning your future all at once, sketching it as a dreamy outline Smiles flashed on the screen, virtual kisses blown to each other Making one smile on the blushing and shy cuteness of another Texting each other words of affirmation imagined as voices And exploring each other all over again as though novices A small tear shed that cant be wiped by the person virtually What starts as mere attraction becomes love, gradually Where love exists, all virtual barriers seem irrelevant For every topic with your partner, you are free to vent A partner who stays with you despite being far away As they always say, where there's a will, there is a way May love find its way back to you, hopefully, one day May life’s surprise be so big that you have nothing to say For my love in a far-off land, he continues to live And only memories are what we have to relive His presence, even in an unknown country or place It gives me peace somehow through my internet space Now I smile, closing the album of our years awaited love story Which now, for our coming generations, has become history Finally, we had made it to the end, no longer virtual but in real All the wounds and pains we suffered, now only time will heal For this is not the end but the beginning of something new Not all can make it up to here, only a selected handful few I miss the old us sometimes; God knows where they went Maybe just for our love story, they were handpicked and sent We had our fair share of love and arguments each time we met But we knew these arguments and life, together made us set Just like it worked out for us maybe it might work out for you The key being loyal for each other and to yourself always be true I bid adieu to you as I embark on my next journey as a poet Everyone’s love eventually works, I’m sure you’ll know it! ~ Shwetangi Srivastava
Original poetry · Shwetangi Srivastava

A fire he was when our eyes first met…

A fire he was when our eyes first met… A smile of his ice easily could melt… Something special he had in those eyes… There lay answers for the what, when and whys. Life lagged in appreciating his beauty… Nor did he realise he was a cutie… His past kept holding him back from trust… For him, there lay only love and no lust… But maybe he’ll find his one someday… In a thorn bed of unrequitedness, I shall lay… I still have those letters of unrequitedness stored… Where evident it was that in love for me he was bored… Where do I find again a man as divine as him? Whose smile makes the city’s lighting seem dim… Why is this ambiguous grief for him there? Even though I’ve lost him before too Isn’t grief for your relief, meant to be share? So that life doesn’t alone beat you black and blue? I shall never love or trust a mortal again… I’d rather die than in unrequited love pain… If he doesn’t come back I’ll sacrifice him gladly… And this story has no happy ending sadly… ~ Shwetangi Srivastava
