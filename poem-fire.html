A Fire
He Was

Poetry · Emotional Storytelling

A fire he was when our eyes first met…

A smile of his ice easily could melt…

Something special he had in those eyes…

There lay answers for the what, when and whys.

Life lagged in appreciating his beauty…

Nor did he realise he was a cutie…

His past kept holding him back from trust…

For him, there lay only love and no lust…

But maybe he’ll find his one someday…

In a thorn bed of unrequitedness, I shall lay…

I still have those letters of unrequitedness stored…

Where evident it was that in love for me he was bored…

Where do I find again a man as divine as him?

Whose smile makes the city’s lighting seem dim…

Why is this ambiguous grief for him there?

Even though I’ve lost him before too

Isn’t grief for your relief, meant to be share?

So that life doesn’t alone beat you black and blue?

I shall never love or trust a mortal again…

I’d rather die than in unrequited love pain…

If he doesn’t come back I’ll sacrifice him gladly…

And this story has no happy ending sadly…

~ Shwetangi Srivastava
